Resolve external identifiers by exact value and support every DrugCentral
crosswalk vocabulary, including PubChem, ChEMBL, UMLS, KEGG, INN, IUPHAR,
ChEBI, and Inxight. UNII values remain case-insensitive, source identifier
spellings are preserved, and `cd_id` is no longer documented as a PubChem ID.
